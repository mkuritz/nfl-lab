📊 Power Index

One 0–100 rating per team for the 2026 season.

Inputs and weights: offense 40% (passing and rushing yards gained, ranked league-wide), defense 40% (passing and rushing yards allowed, ranked) and schedule strength 20% (average offense + defense rank of opponents faced). Wins, losses and points are not inputs, so a 3-2 team that moves the ball well against tough opponents can rank above a 5-0 team.
