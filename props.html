Hit chance = our model chance to clear the line (Anytime TD: TD model v2 with red-zone and goal-line share, blended 75% with the market when a fair price exists; TD props are not Highlighted while the model is under audit; yards/receptions: blended last-10 + season hit rate). Edge = our chance − books' no-vig fair chance, shown only with 2+ books or the sharp group. Matchup = opponent's allowed per game to the position vs league average. Highlighted = edge ≥ 3 pts, or hit chance ≥ 60% with 6+ of last 10 over.
