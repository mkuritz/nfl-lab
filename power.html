Sports Labs Power Rankings

Rating = net EPA per game (offense − defense + special teams) vs an average team, from public play-by-play; roughly points per game. QB = QB EPA per game. SOS = average rating of opponents. Projections = 10,000 season sims using these ratings (13.5-pt game sd, 1.5-pt home edge); win conference is a rating-weighted playoff estimate. Team ratings are Sports Labs ratings (0–100 percentile vs the league) built from EPA, success rate, sack and QB-hit rates. They are not film grades.
