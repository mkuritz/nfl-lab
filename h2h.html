🤝 Head-to-Head Prop Advantage

Every current player with games against tonight's opponent, regular season and playoffs since 2012, with any team. "Injury-adj. proj." is a projection, not a result: his season average plus a share of targets and carries left by teammates who are out (fresh absences only), plus 3% per regular opposing defensive back (passing) or front-seven player (rushing) who is out, capped at 10%. "Prop advantage" marks a market where both his head-to-head average (2+ games) and the injury-adjusted projection are above the current line. It is a comparison only and does not change Highlighted plays. Injury status comes from the latest reports plus team-confirmed absences; a player is only shown Active when the report says so.
